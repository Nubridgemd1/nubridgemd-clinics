Becoming a nurse practitioner is hard enough. Finding a collaborating physician, managing agreements, and staying compliant across state lines shouldn't be.

Meet NuBridgeMD — the platform that connects nurse practitioners and physician assistants with collaborating physicians, and keeps your entire collaboration running in one place. Empowering care, simplified.

Getting started takes three simple steps. Tell us about your practice. Get matched with a collaborating physician. Then sign your agreement and start practicing — all online.

Whether you're a nurse practitioner, a physician assistant, a collaborating physician, or an entire clinic, onboarding is guided and fast. Choose your path on Get Started, add your details and documents, and you're set.

NuBridgeMD matches you with a licensed collaborating physician in your specialty and your state — across all fifty states. Practicing in more than one state? Add coverage anytime.

Your collaborative agreement is generated to your state's rules and signed right in the platform — by you and your physician — with a full audit trail. No printing, no scanning, no chasing signatures.

Choose the plan that fits where you are. StartUp, for brand-new NPs. Basic, for everyday collaboration. And Full-Scope, for complete prescribing, including Schedule 2. Simple monthly pricing — no hidden fees, no surprises.

Running a group practice? With Clinic and Group-Practice accounts, one administrator manages every nurse practitioner — and pays once, by bank transfer. One consolidated payment, every provider in one dashboard, your whole practice streamlined.

Need to get credentialed? NuBridgeMD handles payer enrollment end to end — commercial payers, Medicare and Medicaid — including CAQH, NPI, and re-credentialing.

Payments are effortless. Pay securely by bank transfer or card, turn on automatic monthly billing, and get a receipt every time — all tracked in your dashboard.

Day to day, everything lives together: secure messaging with your physician, shared documents and e-signatures, monthly collaborative check-ins, case consultations, and a task tracker to keep it all on course.

And if you love NuBridgeMD, our referral partner program rewards you for every provider you bring on board.
