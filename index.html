Up and running in three steps

Create your clinic account

Add your nurse practitioners

Enter each NP's details, or send an invite link they use to self‑onboard and link to your clinic.

Pay once by bank transfer

Review the consolidated quote and authorize a single ACH payment. Coverage starts for the whole roster.

Simple, consolidated pricing

A one‑time group registration fee based on clinic size, plus each NP's monthly collaboration tier — billed together as one recurring ACH payment.

Group registration (one‑time)

  • 1–5 nurse practitioners $500
  • 6–10 nurse practitioners $1,000
  • 11+ nurse practitioners $1,500

Charged only on newly‑registered NPs. Providers already registered on NuBridgeMD are grandfathered — no second registration fee.

Per‑NP monthly tier

  • StartUp $350
  • Basic $600
  • Full‑Scope $850

Each NP keeps their own collaboration tier; the clinic's single recurring payment equals the sum of every provider's tier.
